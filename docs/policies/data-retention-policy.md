**Document Owner:** Romeo & Thomas (Co-founders, Caberu)
**Classification:** Internal -- Confidential
**Effective Date:** 2026-02-10
**Last Reviewed:** 2026-02-10
**Review Cycle:** Annual (next review: 2027-02-10)
**Applicable Regulation:** GDPR (EU 2016/679), Belgian Data Protection Act (30 July 2018), Belgian Code of Medical Ethics, Belgian Tax Code (CIR/92)

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the retention periods for all categories of personal data processed by the Caberu dental practice management platform. It ensures compliance with the GDPR's storage limitation principle (Article 5(1)(e)) while respecting minimum retention obligations imposed by Belgian healthcare and tax legislation.

Data must not be kept longer than necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, unless a legal obligation requires longer retention.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all personal data processed through the Caberu platform on behalf of Belgian dental practices, including:

- Patient health records and clinical data
- Appointment and scheduling data
- Billing and payment records
- Communications (SMS, voice calls, chat)
- AI-generated transcripts and summaries
- System logs and audit trails
- Anonymised and aggregated analytics

### 3.2 Detailed Retention Rules

#### 3.2.1 Call Recordings (30 days)

- **Description:** Audio recordings of voice calls made through the platform's ElevenLabs-powered voice AI system.
- **Legal Basis:** Explicit consent of the patient (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). Recordings are only made when the patient provides affirmative consent at the start of the call.
- **Retention:** 30 days from the date of recording.
- **Justification:** Sufficient for quality assurance and dispute resolution. Belgian healthcare law does not require retention of call recordings.
- **After Expiry:** Permanently deleted from Supabase Storage and any associated metadata removed from the database.

#### 3.2.2 Voice Messages (30 days)

- **Description:** Voicemail messages left by patients or practice staff.
- **Legal Basis:** Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
- **Retention:** 30 days from the date the message was left.
- **After Expiry:** Permanently deleted.

#### 3.2.3 AI Transcripts (90 days)

- **Description:** Transcriptions generated by the dental AI chat (`dental-ai-chat` Edge Function), appointment AI assistant, and voice-to-text processing.
- **Legal Basis:** Legitimate interest of the practice in maintaining a record of AI-assisted interactions for quality control and continuity of care (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Retention:** 90 days from creation.
- **Justification:** Provides sufficient time for clinical review and incorporation of relevant information into patient records. Clinically significant information should be transferred to the patient's permanent record within this window.
- **After Expiry:** Permanently deleted. Any clinically relevant content that was manually added to the patient record is retained under the patient records policy.

#### 3.2.4 Chat Messages (90 days)

- **Description:** In-platform messages between patients and practice staff.
- **Legal Basis:** Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for operational communications; contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) for appointment-related communications.
- **Retention:** 90 days from the date of the last message in the conversation thread.
- **After Expiry:** Permanently deleted.

#### 3.2.5 Appointment History (7 years)

- **Description:** Records of all scheduled, completed, cancelled, and no-show appointments, including date, time, practitioner, treatment type, and notes.
- **Legal Basis:** Legal obligation under Belgian healthcare legislation (Art. 6(1)(c)). The Belgian Patient Rights Act and medical record-keeping requirements mandate retention of healthcare records.
- **Retention:** 7 years from the date of the appointment.
- **After Expiry:** Permanently deleted unless subject to a legal hold.

#### 3.2.6 Patient Records (2 years after last interaction)

- **Description:** Patient demographic data, contact information, medical history, treatment plans, clinical notes, imaging, and documents stored in the patient profile.
- **Legal Basis:** Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) during active care; legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) for the minimum retention period.
- **Retention:** Retained for the duration of active care, plus 2 years after the patient's last recorded interaction with the practice.
- **Note:** Belgian law requires retention of patient medical files for a minimum of 30 years for hospital records. For private dental practices, the standard is to retain for the duration of care plus a reasonable period. The 2-year post-interaction period aligns with the standard limitation period for professional liability claims. Practices may configure longer retention if their legal counsel advises it.
- **After Expiry:** Records are flagged for manual review by the practice administrator. The administrator may choose to extend retention, anonymise the record, or confirm deletion.

#### 3.2.7 Billing Records (7 years)

- **Description:** Invoices, payment records, subscription data, insurance claims, and financial transaction history. Payment card details are not stored by Caberu (handled by Stripe).
- **Legal Basis:** Legal obligation under Belgian tax law (Art. 6(1)(c)). Article 315 of the Belgian Income Tax Code (CIR/92) requires retention of accounting documents for 7 years.
- **Retention:** 7 years from the date of the transaction.
- **After Expiry:** Permanently deleted.

#### 3.2.8 Audit Logs (7 years)

- **Description:** Records from `gdpr_audit_log`, `super_admin_audit_log`, and `security_audit_logs` tables.
- **Legal Basis:** Legal obligation to demonstrate GDPR compliance (Art. 5(2) accountability principle) and legitimate interest in security monitoring (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Retention:** 7 years from the date of the log entry.
- **Justification:** Aligns with the maximum retention period for related data (billing, appointments) and ensures audit trails are available for the full period during which the underlying data exists.
- **After Expiry:** Permanently deleted.

#### 3.2.9 Anonymised Statistics (Indefinite)

- **Description:** Aggregated, anonymised analytics data used for platform performance monitoring, business intelligence, and trend analysis. Data is irreversibly anonymised such that no individual can be identified.
- **Legal Basis:** Not applicable. Anonymised data is not personal data within the meaning of the GDPR (Recital 26).
- **Retention:** Indefinite.
- **Standards:** Anonymisation is performed using aggregation with a minimum group size of 10 and removal of all direct and indirect identifiers. The anonymisation process is reviewed annually to ensure it meets the standard of irreversibility.

## 4. Deletion Procedures

### 4.1 Automated Deletion

An automated deletion job runs **daily at 02:00 CET/CEST** to identify and remove data that has exceeded its retention period. The process operates as follows:

1. **Identification:** The job queries each relevant table to identify records where the retention period has elapsed, based on the record's creation date, last interaction date, or transaction date as applicable.

2. **Pre-Deletion Logging:** Before deletion, the job writes a summary record to the `gdpr_audit_log` documenting:
   - Data category
   - Number of records to be deleted
   - Business (tenant) ID
   - Retention rule applied
   - Timestamp of deletion

3. **Deletion Execution:**
   - Database records are permanently deleted using `DELETE` statements (not soft deletes).
   - Associated files in Supabase Storage (recordings, documents, images) are permanently removed.
   - Stripe-side data follows Stripe's own retention policies; Caberu removes its local references.

4. **Post-Deletion Verification:** The job confirms successful deletion and logs the completion status.

5. **Error Handling:** If a deletion batch fails, the error is logged, an alert is sent to the platform administrators, and the batch is retried in the next daily run.

### 4.2 Manual Deletion (Data Subject Requests)

When a patient exercises their right to erasure (Article 17 GDPR):

1. The practice administrator initiates the deletion request through the platform's GDPR tools.
2. The system identifies all data associated with the patient across all tables.
3. Data subject to legal retention obligations (e.g., billing records for 7 years) is **excluded** from deletion and the data subject is informed of the legal basis for continued retention.
4. All other data is permanently deleted within **30 days** of the verified request.
5. A record of the erasure request and the actions taken is retained in the `gdpr_audit_log` (without the deleted personal data) for accountability purposes.

### 4.3 Tenant Offboarding

When a dental practice terminates its use of the Caberu platform:

1. All data belonging to the tenant is exported and provided to the practice in a portable format (Article 20).
2. After confirmation of receipt (or after a 90-day grace period), all tenant data is permanently deleted.
3. Anonymised statistical data may be retained as it is no longer personal data.

## 5. Anonymisation Procedures

Where data is anonymised rather than deleted (e.g., for statistical purposes), the following standards apply:

1. **Aggregation:** Individual records are combined into groups of no fewer than 10 to prevent identification.
2. **Identifier Removal:** All direct identifiers (name, email, phone, national register number) and indirect identifiers (date of birth, address, unique appointment patterns) are removed.
3. **Verification:** The anonymised dataset is reviewed to confirm that re-identification is not reasonably possible, considering all means likely to be used.
4. **Documentation:** The anonymisation methodology is documented and retained.

## 6. Legal Hold Exceptions

In the following circumstances, the standard retention periods are overridden and data is preserved until the hold is lifted:

1. **Pending or Anticipated Litigation:** If the practice or Caberu is involved in, or reasonably anticipates, legal proceedings related to specific data.
2. **Regulatory Investigation:** If a supervisory authority (e.g., the Belgian DPA -- APD/GBA) requests preservation of data in connection with an investigation.
3. **Insurance Claims:** If data is relevant to an open insurance claim or professional liability matter.
4. **Law Enforcement Request:** If a valid legal order requires data preservation.

**Procedure for Legal Holds:**

- Legal holds are initiated by the practice administrator or Caberu's co-founders.
- The hold is recorded in the system, specifying the scope (which data, which patients, which time period) and the reason.
- Automated deletion jobs skip data covered by an active legal hold.
- Legal holds are reviewed every 90 days to determine whether they remain necessary.
- When a legal hold is lifted, the data resumes its normal retention schedule (the retention clock does not restart).
