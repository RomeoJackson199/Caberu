Caberu SRL ("Caberu", "we", "us", or "our") operates an AI-powered dental practice management platform that helps Belgian healthcare providers manage appointments, patient records, billing, communications, and clinical workflows.

In the context of this Privacy Policy:

- When a **dental practice** uses Caberu to manage patient data, the dental practice is the **Data Controller** and Caberu acts as the **Data Processor** under a separate Data Processing Agreement (DPA).
- When Caberu collects data directly from **practice administrators, dentists, and staff** who use the platform (account holders), Caberu acts as a **Data Controller** for that account and usage data.

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle personal data in both capacities.

**Data Protection Officer (DPO):** For all privacy-related inquiries, contact our DPO at [Romeo@caberu.be](mailto:Romeo@caberu.be).

## 2. What Data We Collect

### 2.1 Data We Collect as Data Controller (Platform Users)

When dentists, practice administrators, or staff create an account and use Caberu, we collect:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Account information** | Full name, email address, phone number, professional qualifications, practice name and address |
| **Authentication data** | Hashed passwords, two-factor authentication tokens, session identifiers |

### 2.2 Data We Process as Data Processor (Patient Data)

When dental practices use Caberu to manage their patients, the following categories of patient data may be processed on behalf of the practice (as Controller):

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Identity data** | Patient name, date of birth, national register number (if provided by practice) |
| **Contact data** | Phone number, email address, postal address |
| **Health and medical data** | Medical history, dental records, treatment plans, prescriptions, allergies, clinical notes, X-ray and imaging files |

**Special category data:** Health and medical data constitute special category data under Article 9 of the GDPR. This data is processed solely on the instructions of the dental practice (Data Controller) under the legal bases described in Section 3.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

### 3.1 As Data Controller (Platform Users)

We rely on the following legal bases under Article 6(1) GDPR:

| Legal Basis | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Contract performance** (Art. 6(1)(b)) | Providing the Caberu platform, managing your account, processing subscription payments, delivering customer support |
| **Legitimate interest** (Art. 6(1)(f)) | Improving our platform, security monitoring, fraud prevention, aggregated analytics to enhance service quality. We balance these interests against your rights and freedoms. |
| **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c)) | Tax and accounting obligations under Belgian law, responding to lawful requests from authorities |
| **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) | Marketing communications, optional analytics cookies, participation in beta features. You may withdraw consent at any time. |

### 3.2 As Data Processor (Patient Data)

When processing patient data on behalf of dental practices, we act strictly on the Controller's documented instructions as set out in our Data Processing Agreement.

The dental practice (Controller) is responsible for establishing its own legal basis for processing patient data. Typical legal bases include:

- **Article 9(2)(h) GDPR:** Processing necessary for the provision of healthcare, subject to Belgian professional secrecy obligations (Article 458 Belgian Criminal Code)
- **Article 6(1)(b) GDPR:** Performance of the treatment contract between the practice and the patient
- **Article 6(1)(c) GDPR:** Compliance with Belgian healthcare record-keeping obligations (Royal Decree of 3 May 1999; Law of 22 August 2002 on Patient Rights)

## 4. How We Use Your Data

### 4.1 Platform Users (Controller Capacity)

We use your data to:

- **Provide and maintain the platform** -- account management, authentication, feature delivery
- **Process payments** -- subscription billing, invoicing, refunds
- **Communicate with you** -- service announcements, security alerts, support responses
- **Improve our services** -- analyze usage patterns (aggregated), develop new features, fix bugs
- **Ensure security** -- detect and prevent unauthorized access, monitor for abuse
- **Comply with the law** -- fulfill tax obligations, respond to legal requests

### 4.2 Patient Data (Processor Capacity)

On instruction from dental practices, we process patient data to:

- **Manage appointments** -- scheduling, reminders via SMS/email, cancellation handling
- **Maintain dental records** -- store and organize treatment plans, clinical notes, imaging
- **Enable communications** -- AI-powered phone handling, voice messages, SMS notifications
- **Support billing** -- generate invoices, track payments, manage insurance claims
- **Provide AI assistance** -- AI dental chat support, appointment recommendations, voice transcription (always assistive, never diagnostic)
- **Generate analytics** -- practice performance dashboards, patient flow statistics (presented to the Controller only)

## 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including:

**Technical Measures:**
- Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Row Level Security (RLS) enforced at the database level -- each practice can only access its own data
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) support for all user accounts
- Automated vulnerability scanning and dependency monitoring
- Input sanitization (DOMPurify) to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS)
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with principle of least privilege
- Automated session management and token expiration
- Regular backups with encryption

**Organizational Measures:**
- Staff background checks and confidentiality agreements
- Data protection training for all personnel
- Access limited to personnel who require it for their role
- Incident response plan with defined procedures (see our Incident Response Plan)
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Console logging stripped from production builds to prevent data leakage

## 8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Our detailed retention schedule is published in our Data Retention Policy. Key retention periods:

| Data Type | Retention Period | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Call recordings | 30 days | Operational necessity |
| Voice messages | 30 days | Operational necessity |
| AI transcripts | 90 days | Service quality, dispute resolution |
| Chat messages | 90 days | Service quality |
| Appointment history | 7 years | Belgian healthcare record-keeping |
| Patient health records | 30 years from last entry | Belgian medical records law (Royal Decree) |
| Patient contact data | 2 years after last interaction (unless health records retention applies) | Legitimate interest, contract performance |
| Billing and payment records | 7 years | Belgian tax law (Article 315 CIR/WIB) |
| Audit logs | 7 years | Security, legal compliance |
| Anonymized/aggregated statistics | Indefinite | No personal data involved |
| Account data (platform users) | Duration of account + 2 years | Contract performance, legal obligations |

When data reaches the end of its retention period, it is securely deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

## 9. Your Rights

### 9.1 If You Are a Platform User (Dentist/Staff)

As a data subject under the GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to data we control:

| Right | Description |
|---|---|
| **Access** (Art. 15) | Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you |
| **Rectification** (Art. 16) | Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data |
| **Erasure** (Art. 17) | Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal retention obligations |
| **Restriction** (Art. 18) | Request that we limit how we process your data |
| **Data portability** (Art. 20) | Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format |
| **Object** (Art. 21) | Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing |
| **Withdraw consent** (Art. 7(3)) | Withdraw any consent you have given, at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing |
| **Automated decision-making** (Art. 22) | Not be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or significant effects. Caberu's AI features are assistive and do not make autonomous clinical or legal decisions. |

**How to exercise your rights:** Send a request to [Romeo@caberu.be](mailto:Romeo@caberu.be). We will verify your identity and respond within **30 days** (extendable by 60 days for complex requests, with notice). There is no fee for exercising your rights unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

### 9.2 If You Are a Patient

If you are a patient of a dental practice that uses Caberu, the practice is the Data Controller of your data. Please direct your rights requests to your dental practice in the first instance.

We will assist the dental practice in fulfilling your rights requests as described in our Data Processing Agreement.
